#1 Most Useful

A real-time 3D sound path that updates the moment you change probe angle, frequency, or wedge delay — alongside the live A-scan waveform, spread angle, and near-field length. Free for everyone, no unlock required.

AWS D1.1

AWS D-Rating, step by step.

Walk through indication length, amplitude, and D-value with built-in AWS D1.1 and ASME terminology — Clause 8 logic on-device, ready when the call goes out.

DAC / TCG

Build reference curves on site.

Plot DAC and TCG curves from your reflector points and get a usable gain table in seconds. No laptop, no spreadsheet — your iPhone is the calibrator.

Field workflow

Locate flaws faster.

Type a sound path, get depth and projection instantly. The 3-number answer most field workflows are based on, surfaced in one tap.

OCR Capture

Scan the screen. Parameters fill in.

Point at the probe's instrument display and let OCR map velocity, MHz and dB into the parameter card in seconds — fewer typos, faster setup.

Material library

Steel, stainless, aluminum — preset.

Built-in material presets for the metals you'll meet on site. One tap to load velocity and acoustic properties; custom materials supported.

A typical workflow

From reading to report,
in one app.

  1. 01

    Set up the probe.

    Pick a material preset, dial angle, frequency. The 3D beam shows you exactly where L1 and L2 will land.

  2. 02

    Scan & identify.

    Use the OCR scanner to read values off your instrument, or enter them by hand. Flaw location returns depth and projection immediately.

  3. 03

    Apply the code.

    AWS D-Rating and DAC/TCG wizard steps you through the assessment with the right terminology on-screen.

  4. 04

    Export & archive.

    Generate a PDF or CSV report right on the device. Hand it to the client, file it in your records, done.

FAQ

Frequently asked.

Does it work offline?

Yes — fully. NDT Master runs entirely on your iPhone. No account, no cloud, no upload. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro is a single one-time purchase ($9.99). A 7-day free trial is included. No auto-renew, no recurring fees.

Which codes does it support?

AWS D1.1 indication rating (Clause 8) and ASME terminology. DAC/TCG follows standard reference-block workflows.

Does it support iPad?

Yes — a native iPad layout with the same screens, optimised for the larger display.

How many languages?

35 languages, including Arabic, Hindi, Hebrew, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, German, French, and many more.

Can I export inspection reports?

Yes — export to PDF or CSV right on the device. Files stay local until you choose to share or print.
