Buddhist Widget is an iOS app and home / lock screen widget for daily Buddhist scripture quotes in your preferred language. The app offers a 7-day trial and a one-time Pro unlock (com.linxue.fowidget.unlock.pro) that enables additional themes and raises the favorites limit.

Not a subscription: the Pro unlock is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription, no auto-renewal, and no recurring charge. The 7-day trial is computed locally on your device and never converts into a payment.

Important: When contacting support, please do not send screenshots of your purchase receipt, Apple ID, Keychain contents, App Group container, or device identifiers. Only describe the issue and the app version.

FAQ

How do I add Buddhist Widget to the Home Screen or Lock Screen?

Open the in-app Widget Hub, choose a size, and follow the system instructions to add a WidgetKit widget. On iPhone, long-press the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Buddhist Widget. iOS controls the final placement, size, and refresh behaviour.

Why does the widget show the same quote every day?

WidgetKit controls when widgets refresh. The app writes a timeline with the next quotes, and iOS decides when to render a new entry based on system load, battery, and your viewing habits. To force a refresh, open the companion app once so it can update the App Group data, then reopen the widget.

How do I change the quote language?

Open the app's Settings → Content language and pick a content language (English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, or follow the interface language). The widget will pick up the change the next time it refreshes. Interface language follows iOS Settings unless you override it in Settings → Interface language.

Why does the content language fall back to English?

The current release ships with English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Japanese public-domain quote content. Interface languages such as German, French, or Spanish fall back to the English content language because no public-domain translation is bundled for those languages.

How do I switch themes?

Open the app's Settings → Theme and pick from the available themes. Some themes are part of the Pro unlock. Themes affect both the companion app and the widget, subject to WidgetKit refresh.

What does the Pro unlock include?

The one-time Pro unlock (com.linxue.fowidget.unlock.pro) enables the Pro theme set and raises the favorites limit from 20 to 999. It does not unlock additional content languages, additional quote libraries, or cloud sync. Content language availability depends on the bundled public-domain translations.

How long is the trial and how does it work?

The trial starts the first time you open the app and lasts 7 days. During the trial, Pro themes and the higher favorites limit are unlocked without purchase. The trial start date is kept in the iOS Keychain (service com.linxue.fowidget.entitlements), which means uninstalling and reinstalling the app does not reset the trial window.

How do I restore the Pro purchase?

Open the app's Settings → Restore purchase. The app reads the local StoreKit AppTransaction to verify the Pro entitlement for the signed-in Apple ID. Restore requires the same Apple ID that originally completed the purchase. Refunds, billing issues, and family sharing questions are handled by Apple through your purchase history.

Why did my favorites stop saving?

The free version keeps up to 20 favorites. When the favorites limit is reached, adding a new favorite is blocked so the list stays predictable. Pro raises the limit to 999. Removing items from the favorites list frees room for new ones.

Where does the quote content come from?

Quote content is bundled in quotes.json from public-domain sources: SuttaCentral English translations (Dhammapada), CBETA Simplified and Traditional Chinese (Dhammapada and other well-known scriptures), and Aozora / Wikisource Japanese (荻原雲來訳『法句經』 and selected Japanese Buddhist texts). No additional quote content is downloaded at runtime.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No in the current build. Quote text, language preferences, theme preferences, favorites, recent quotes, trial state, and purchase state are all stored on device inside the app and the App Group container. The app does not operate a server for collecting usage data, and the PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types.

What should I send when reporting a bug?

Send the app version, device, iOS or iPadOS version, the language and widget setup, the steps to reproduce, and sanitized screenshots. Do not send Apple ID screenshots, purchase receipts, Keychain data, App Group container paths, or device identifiers.
