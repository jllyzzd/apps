Support

App: Buddhist Widget — daily Buddhist quotes with beautiful Home Screen widgets and a calm reading view.

Before contacting us: most questions are answered below. The app works entirely offline, collects no data, and requires no account.

Frequently Asked Questions

The widget shows no content or says it needs a refresh.

Widgets read a small local snapshot that the app refreshes in the background. Open the app once, then remove and re-add the widget. If it still misbehaves, restart your device — this rebuilds the widget system.

Which languages is the content available in?

The quote library is available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese. You can switch the content track inside the app at any time. The app interface itself supports more languages (including German, French and Spanish).

Why can I only save a few favorites?

The free version allows up to 10 favorites. The one-time Pro unlock raises this limit to 999 and also unlocks all premium visual themes (21 themes in total; the free version includes 1).

I bought Pro but my purchase is not showing.

Pro purchases are handled by Apple. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID used for the purchase, then use the Restore Purchases option in the app. If restoration fails, check your purchase history in the App Store and try again on a stable connection.

Is my data synced or uploaded anywhere?

No. The app has no network capability. Favorites, settings and reading progress are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. See our Privacy Policy.

How to Report a Problem

When writing to us, please include the following so we can help you faster:

  1. Device model and iOS version (Settings > General > About).
  2. The app version (shown on the app's settings screen).
  3. What you expected, what happened instead, and whether the issue happens in the app itself, in the widget, or both.
