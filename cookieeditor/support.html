FAQ

How do I enable Cookie Lens in Safari?

Open the app and follow the Safari extension setup guide. In Safari, enable the extension and allow it for the site where you want to inspect cookies. Safari controls the final site permission prompt and settings.

Why does Cookie Lens need site access?

Site access lets the Safari extension read and manage cookies for the current site where you open the extension. Cookie Lens uses this access for current-site cookie management, not for advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking.

Why do I not see all cookies?

Safari, WebKit, HttpOnly, security, and site-specific restrictions can prevent some cookies from being visible or editable. Cookie Lens shows cookies that are accessible to the Safari extension for the current site and does not bypass browser security restrictions.

Does Cookie Lens upload my cookies?

No in the current release. Cookie viewing, editing, import, export, and Header generation are processed on device. Cookie Lens does not operate a developer server for collecting cookie data.

Where are exported JSON files saved?

Exported files are created only when you choose to export. After that, the file location and sharing destination are controlled by you and by iOS. Please protect exported files because they can contain sensitive session data.

What happens when I copy a Cookie Header string?

The Header string is copied to the system clipboard after your action. Clipboard content may be visible to other apps or system features depending on iOS behavior, so clear or replace it when you no longer need it.

Why did a JSON import partially fail?

Some records may be skipped or fail validation because of invalid fields, unsupported formats, site permission limits, domain/path mismatch, Safari restrictions, or cookie attributes that cannot be written in the current context.

Can Cookie Lens edit HttpOnly cookies?

HttpOnly and other restricted cookies may be hidden or protected by Safari and WebKit. Cookie Lens explains those limits but does not bypass them.

What should I send when reporting a bug?

Send the app version, device, iOS or iPadOS version, Safari site type, sanitized steps, and screenshots with sensitive values removed. Do not send real cookies, active login sessions, full Header strings, or exported JSON containing private data.
