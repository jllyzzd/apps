Questions

Good to know

Which medications does it support?
Any injectable or oral medication — research peptides, GLP-1 pens, daily tablets. Doses in mg, mcg, units or IU.
Can I track more than one medication?
Yes — each with its own plan, schedule and history. Free includes one; unlocking adds more.
Is this a subscription?
No. Free for 14 days, then one purchase if you stay. When the trial ends nothing is deleted or locked, and reminders keep working.
Where is my data stored?
On your device and your own private iCloud. No account, never on our servers.
Does it tell me what dose to take?
No. It records what you enter — it doesn't recommend doses. Follow your clinician's instructions.
