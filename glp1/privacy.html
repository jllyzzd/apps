Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

App: Peptide Tracker: Shots & Pills

Bundle ID: com.linxue.glp1

Short version: Peptide Tracker has no account, no ads and no trackers. Your medication records stay on your device and, if you enable iCloud, in your own private iCloud. They are never uploaded to our servers — we do not operate any server that could receive them. The app's App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

1. Data We Collect

In the current release, Peptide Tracker does not collect any personal data from your device. Specifically:

  • No account, name, email address, phone number or physical address — there is no sign-up or sign-in screen.
  • No upload of medication plans, dose records, injection sites, body reactions, weight, hydration or mood entries to developer servers.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA), no App Tracking Transparency prompt, no cross-app tracking.
  • No third-party analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.

This behavior is intended to align with Data Not Collected in the App Store privacy labeling.

2. What the App Stores on Your Device

Everything you enter is stored locally in the app container on your device:

  • Medication plans and dose schedules (including injectable and oral routes).
  • Dose records, dates and times, and injection-site history from the body map.
  • Body reactions, weight, hydration and mood entries.
  • App settings such as language, appearance, units and reminder preferences.
  • Small summary snapshots (for the Home Screen widgets and the Apple Watch app), stored in a shared app-group container on your device.

You can delete any record inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes this data from the device. Files you have exported (see Section 5) must be deleted from their destination separately.

3. iCloud Sync

If iCloud sync is enabled, your records are stored in your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) and synced between your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

  • Sync goes device-to-Apple's-iCloud only. The developer has no access to your iCloud data and operates no server in this flow.
  • Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.
  • You can turn sync off in iOS Settings, or delete the app's iCloud data from Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

4. Permissions

Peptide Tracker requests only the permissions needed for features you choose to use:

  • Notifications (optional): used for local dose reminders that are scheduled on your device. Declining notifications does not affect logging or any other feature.
  • iCloud (optional): used only if you enable sync, as described in Section 3.
  • The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or full photo library access.

5. Data Sharing and Export

The app never automatically sends your records anywhere. Data leaves the app only when you explicitly choose to:

  • Export a PDF report, CSV file or full archive, or share records via the iOS Share Sheet, AirDrop, Files or email.

Once you share data with another person, app or service, that destination's own privacy policy applies.

6. Purchases

The unlock purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives your payment details, and does not receive your Apple ID or any identifier from the transaction. Family Sharing and purchase restoration are handled by Apple's App Store infrastructure.

7. Tracking, Analytics, and Advertising

The current release contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, no third-party crash-reporting SDKs and no tracking of any kind. The app does not access your precise or approximate location.

8. Health Information

Medication records, body reactions and weight are sensitive by nature. This is exactly why they are kept on your device and in your personal iCloud, and never transmitted to the developer. The app is not a medical device, does not connect to Apple Health, and does not share health data with third parties.

9. Children's Privacy

Peptide Tracker is a personal medication-tracking tool intended for adults managing their own prescribed or self-administered treatment. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Policy Updates

If the app's privacy behavior changes in a future release — for example, if an account system or analytics were ever introduced — this page will be updated with a new effective date, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match before that release ships.
